Pet Village

Player's guide

Adopt a pet, keep them fed and happy, earn coins around the village, and decorate a house — with other penguins wandering the same streets.

Getting started

Sign in with Google or email for cloud saves and multiplayer, or hit Continue as guest to play the whole single-player game on local saves.

Then adopt a pet: pick from the Tamagotchi cast (Mametchi, Kuchipatchi, Mimitchi and friends), the mascot friends, or a Puffle in ten colours — then give them a name. That name is yours: once you've adopted, no other player can take it.

You start in Town as a penguin with your pet trotting behind you. Walk with WASD / arrows or click where you want to go. Anything you can use — doors, shop counters, game booths, villagers, your pet — shows a prompt when you get close: press E or Space, or just click it.

Your pet

Three needs sit at the top of the screen and decay in real time — even while the game is closed, capped at 12 hours away so a week off isn't a punishment. Per hour: food −6, happy −4, energy −3.

Need How to fill it
Food Feed a snack from the pet menu (P) or straight from your inventory (I → Food & treats) — buy snacks at Daniel's Shop or fish for them at the Shore. Each snack shows what it will really add, so you can tell when your pet is too full to bother.
Happy Play a mini-game, chat with your pet, or dress them in clothes from Cafe Cinnamon
Energy Tuck your pet into a bed at home — they wake up at full energy

Energy is what limits play: every mini-game charges it up front, and a pet without enough simply can't be sent in — the booth turns you away, and where a game has difficulties the ones you can't afford are greyed out with the reason. Costs run from 4 (one fishing cast) to 18 (a hard Paper Toss run), so a tired pet needs a nap before the next round. Buy a Dream Bed (50 coins), place it in the house, then stand next to it for the E / Space / click prompt, or click the bed and choose "Tuck in".

The village

  • Town — the hub. Your house, Daniel's Shop and Cafe Cinnamon are here, with signed gates out: West Green to the left, East Green to the right, the Shore at the bottom.
  • The West Green — the Skip Rope booth, the Bump arena and the Sled Run hill.
  • The East Green — the Paper Toss and Get arcade cabinets, and the Expedition duelling tent.
  • The Shore — cast a line and fish for food. Your biggest catch is recorded in centimetres.
  • Daniel's Shop — food, furniture and fishing bait. Talk to Daniel at the counter.
  • Cafe Cinnamon — Cinna's boutique: penguin colourways and clothes for you and your pet.
  • Your house — a decoratable grid, and the only place your pet can sleep.

Villagers wander town and the greens. Say hello — each hands out one small coin gift per day, and Cafe Cinnamon sells Bongbongee outfits for diamond pets. Town's villagers take turns: one heads home and another arrives every minute and a half, so stay a while and you'll meet the whole neighbourhood. Bongbongee never leaves.

Mini-games

Every game charges your pet's energy up front. Harder difficulties cost more and pay more back, and a winning run works out at roughly the same coins per energy at any of the five coin-paying games — so pick the one you enjoy rather than the one that pays best. Longer games cost more: a Paper Toss run is ten throws. Fishing is the odd one out on purpose: it pays fish and happiness instead of coins.

Fish are food, and the rarer the better: Silver Minnow, then Mint Bass, then Sunset Snapper (45 food, 22 happy). None of them are for sale — the Shore is the only source.

Coins & shopping

Daniel's Shop

  • Food — Fishy Snack 5, Choco Cookie 8.
  • Bait — 3 coins a cast.
  • Furniture — Flower Vase 15, Potted Plant 20, Sun Lamp 22, Pink Rug 25, Cozy Chair 30, Wood Table 35, Bookshelf 45, Dream Bed 50, Retro TV 60.

Cafe Cinnamon

  • Penguin colours — twelve colourways for your own penguin.
  • Clothes — hats, held items and outfits, for you and for your pet.

Your house

Buy furniture, then press I at home to open the decorate menu and place it on the grid. Click a placed piece to pick it back up into your inventory — or, if it's a bed, to tuck your pet in.

Playing together

Sign in and the village fills up. Presence is shared across six worlds — Town, the Shore, both Greens, Daniel's Shop and Cafe Cinnamon — so you see everyone in the room with you, pets and all.

  • Wave hello. Click any penguin — if they're across the square your own walks over first and waves once it arrives (click elsewhere to call it off). They get a toast telling them who waved.
  • Dance. Press N — or the bottom-right Dance chip on a phone — for the full classic Club Penguin emote loop (76 frames: spin, arms overhead, waves, tumbles). Everyone in the scene sees it. Press again or start walking to stop. On mobile the Inv / Pet / Chat / Dance chips shrink so they fit beside the joystick.
  • Say something. Press T — or the Chat button next to Pet and Inventory, which raises the keyboard on a phone — type a line, and Enter sends it. It appears in a bubble over your penguin's head for everyone sharing the world, and stays up long enough to read — longer for a longer message. Esc throws the draft away, and so does tapping anywhere else.
  • The chat log in the top-left corner keeps the last few lines after the bubbles have faded, and is where you find out who has joined the village and who has left it. Each line fades on its own once it has been up a while.
  • Names float above everyone — the player's name over the player, the pet's name over the pet — and no two villagers can share one.
  • Players in a mini-game stay in the world, dimmed and labelled with what they're playing, so the streets never look empty.
  • The server owns positions. It grants one spawn per world transition and corrects clients that drift, so nobody teleports around the map.
  • Drop out and come back within the grace window and you return to the world you were in, with no ghost of you left behind.
  • Rename yourself or your pet from the topbar; the new name is re-reserved and pushed to everyone who can see you.

Two known gaps: remote penguins render in their own colourway but not yet in their clothes, and only your Town spot is remembered between sessions — every other world puts you at its portal.

Saves

Signed in, your save lives in the cloud and follows you between devices. As a guest it lives in this browser's localStorage, so clearing site data resets your progress. Either way your pet keeps living while you're away — capped at 12 hours of catching up.
