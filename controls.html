Pet Village

Controls

Walk, care for your pet, decorate your house, and earn coins.

Play the game Game guide

Around the village

Input Action
W A S D / arrows Walk around
Click / tap, or hold Walk to that spot (Club Penguin style)
On-screen joystick Drag it to walk on a phone or tablet
E / Space / click Use whatever is prompting you: door, gate, shop counter, game booth, villager, your pet
P Open the pet menu — feed a snack, play, check needs
Click another penguin Wave hello — from far away you walk over first, then wave
N Dance — classic Club Penguin bounce. Press again or walk to stop
T / the Chat button Say something — type your message, Enter sends it, and it appears over your penguin's head for everyone in the scene, and in the chat log in the corner. Esc throws the draft away, and so does tapping anywhere else. The button sits beside Pet and Inventory at the bottom right; on a phone it raises the keyboard.
I At home: open the decorate menu
Click furniture At home: pick it back up — or tuck your pet into a bed
ESC / click outside Close a menu

In the mini-games

Game Input
Paper Toss Drag back and release to slingshot the ball
Fishing Drag to aim the cast, click when the line dips, then either hold to lift the catch bar or tap in the green — whichever fight the fish gives you
Skip Rope Space / tap to jump
Bump! Wait for the villager's move, then mash Space / tap
Get A D / left + right arrows, or hold on the side you want, to slide the bowl
Expedition Your turn: pick an ability, then Space / tap the arcs as the needle sweeps. Their turn: X / left half to dodge, C / right half to parry (tighter window, grants mana + counter if the whole chain is parried)
Sled Run A D / left + right arrows, or tap either side, to steer
Any game ESC or the back button to bail out

Village tips

  • Your pet follows you everywhere. Food / Happy / Energy decay in real time — including while the game is closed (capped at 12h). Feed snacks, play, and tuck them into a bed to restore energy.
  • Daniel's Shop sells food, fishing bait and furniture. Cafe Cinnamon sells penguin colourways and clothes for you and your pet.
  • The West Green has Skip Rope, Bump! and the Sled Run hill; the East Green has Paper Toss, Get and Expedition; the Shore is for fishing. Every mini-game spends pet energy up front: 5–12 a game on most green booths, 8–21 for Expedition, 10 for a Skip Rope run, 10–18 for a Paper Toss run, and 4 for every single fishing cast. A pet without enough can't be sent in, so keep a Dream Bed at home for naps.
  • Your house is a decoratable grid: buy furniture, place with I, click to pick it back up.
  • Say hello to villagers — each has one small coin gift a day.
