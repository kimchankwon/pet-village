PET VILLAGE

Fishing redo — 3 playable prototypes

Three replacements for the current cast → bite → surge-reel fight. All three are playable right here. Pick one (or mix) and it gets built into FishingScene.ts.

Jump to:

1. Keep It In/ 2. Line Tension/ 3. The Sweep

1. Keep It In — the Stardew bar

Hold to lift the green bar, let go to drop it. Keep the fish inside and the catch meter fills. Lose the fish and it drains.

Hold SPACE or press and hold the mouse / your finger

Why it works

  • One input, zero explanation — everyone reads it in 2 seconds.
  • Difficulty is pure tuning: bar height, fish dartiness, gravity. Rare fish get a short bar and a jumpy fish, no new code.
  • Feels like a fight the whole time, unlike the current one where you wait for surges.

Watch out

  • It is very obviously Stardew Valley. That's fine, but it's not ours.
  • Needs a touch dead-zone — on mobile, holding while the page can scroll is fiddly.

2. Line Tension — pump the rod

Hold to reel in, but the line tightens while you do. Let go before it snaps. The fish surges on its own schedule — reeling through a surge spikes tension fast.

Hold SPACE to reel, release to let the line rest

Why it works

  • Closest to what's already in FishingScene.ts — the surge cycle and tension meter survive, so it's the cheapest to build.
  • Rhythm instead of reflex. It reads as patient and calm, which suits the game more than a twitch test.
  • Telegraphed surges reward learning the fish rather than mashing.

Watch out

  • Two meters to read at once — the current version's main problem, and this keeps it.
  • Optimal play is "hold until amber, release" on repeat, which can get dull once learned.

3. The Sweep — timed strikes

A needle sweeps the dial. Tap when it crosses the green zone to gain line. Every hit speeds the needle up and shrinks the zone. Three misses and the fish is gone.

Tap SPACE or click — timing, not holding

Why it works

  • Discrete taps, so it works identically on phone and desktop — no hold-vs-scroll problem.
  • Escalating tension built in: the last hit of a rare fish is genuinely tense.
  • Perfect hits (dead centre) can pay a bonus — a skill ceiling for repeat players.

Watch out

  • Feels more like a lockpick than a fish. Least "fishing" of the three.
  • Short fights — a common fish is over in ~5 seconds, so casting overhead starts to dominate.

My pick

1. Keep It In, with the surge telegraph from 2 layered on: when the fish is about to dart, it flashes first. That keeps the readable single-meter feel and gets back the "learn this fish" depth the current version was reaching for.

The cast stays as-is either way — the slingshot drag is the good part of the current scene. Only the fight after the bite changes.

Prototypes only — nothing here is wired into the game yet.
