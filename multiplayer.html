Shared worlds

Presence is synchronized across six worlds: Town, the Shore, both Parks, Daniel's Shop, and Café Cinnamon. Walk through a portal and the server moves you with it — every other penguin in that world appears, and you disappear from the one you left.

  • The server owns your position. Each transition grants exactly one approved spawn, so a client cannot teleport itself across the map. If the server disagrees with where you are, it sends a correction: same world and you snap to it, different world and the game switches scenes to match.
  • Remote penguins move smoothly. Positions interpolate at a rate that is stable no matter your frame rate, and anything further than a screen away snaps instead of sliding across the world.
  • Village NPCs are shared. The Town room runs one NPC simulation for everyone, so wanderers are in the same place on every screen.
  • Pets come along. Other players' pets walk with them, named and wearing their accessories.
  • Dropped connections are held. A reconnect within the grace window restores you to the world you were in — no respawn in Town, no ghost left behind.

Saying hello

Input What happens
Click a nearby penguin You wave at them; they get a toast saying who waved
Someone waves near you You see their wave animation, whoever it was aimed at
Walk up to a player Their name floats above them, their pet's name above the pet

Waves are proximity-checked and rate-limited on the server, so a wave you see is a wave that really happened close by.

The wave animation

The penguin art pipeline only draws walk plates, so the wave poses are derived from the real plate art rather than hand-drawn: scripts/penguin-wave-plates.mts finds the viewer's left flipper on down-0.png, keeps its shoulder welded to the body, and swings the rest up into the empty plate beside the head — three poses at 165°, 172° and 180°.

In game those play as a one-shot 0 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 2 → 1 at 130ms per frame, where frame 0 is the idle plate: the flipper snaps up, wiggles, and drops. The plates are full resolution and go through the same runtime recolour as every other pose, so a wave from a pink penguin is pink.

Drop authored poses over wave-{1,2,3}.png and the game picks them up unchanged — nothing in the game reads the generator.

Names that are yours

  • One name each. Adopting a pet reserves your player name and your pet's name in the same transaction as the save, so two penguins can never end up sharing a nametag. Names are compared normalized — Mochi, mochi and ｍＯＣＨＩ are the same name.
  • Renaming from the topbar validates the new name, re-reserves it, and pushes the change to everyone who can see you.
  • Older saves are grandfathered. A duplicate or invalid legacy pet name is migrated to a unique one, and the old name keeps working while another tab is still sending it.
  • Your account name follows your profile. Email sign-ups arrive with no name at all, so the canonical display name is written back to the account and the topbar always agrees with the nametag.

Back home

  • Tuck your pet into bed. Stand near a placed bed for the E / Space / click prompt, or click the bed and pick "Tuck in" — your pet climbs in and wakes up with full energy.
  • Minigame ghosts. A player who ducks into a minigame stays visible in the world, dimmed and labelled with what they are playing, so the village never looks empty.
  • Guests are welcome. "Continue as guest" plays the whole single player game on local saves; signing in adds cloud saves and multiplayer.
Saved positions are Town-only: other worlds put you at their portal spawn. Remote penguins render recoloured but not yet clothed.
